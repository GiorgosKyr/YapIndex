## Short bios

**Nadia Chen — CEO.** Co-founded Meridian after four years as a product lead
at a large retail analytics company. Runs commercial, finance, and people.
Author of the "one customer per week" ritual — every Friday she interviews
one paying customer.

**Ravi Patel — CTO.** Co-founder, wrote the original Django monolith by
himself in the first six months. Now focused on architecture, hiring senior
engineers, and long-horizon bets (currently: EU region, ML anomaly detection).

**Marcus Oduya — VP Engineering.** Joined mid-2023 to grow the org past its
"one big room" phase. Owns delivery, org structure, and eng operations.
Chairs the ADR review committee.

**Priya Ramanathan — Head of Platform.** Runs the 8-person Platform team
plus Ingest (Wen Liu reports in) and interim-leads ML/Insights. Has been
the technical driver of the ECS→EKS migration and the current EU buildout.

**Jae-won Park — Head of Data.** Owns ClickHouse, Kafka, Atlas, and
Cartograph. Led the 2022 ClickHouse adoption and the 2023 "Kraken" cluster
project.

**Elena Vasquez — Head of Product Eng.** Runs the 12-engineer team behind
Portal, Beacon, Lighthouse, Beacon-Aggregator, and Relay. Owns the v3 API
rollout and v2 deprecation.

**Tomás Herrera — Head of Growth Eng.** Owns Ledger, Compass, and the
billing UI in Portal. Managed the response to INC-2025-03-08 and drove the
usage-counters-to-Postgres migration.

**Dara Okonkwo — Head of Security.** Hired after the Series B to build out
Security as a formal function. Runs the 3-person team behind Gatekeeper and
the compliance program (SOC 2 Type II achieved 2025-Q4).

**Sam Whitfield — Head of Product.** Owns product management and design.

**Lin Zhao — Head of Support.** Runs a 9-person support team across Seattle
and London hubs. Owns customer-facing runbooks and the escalation path into
engineering on-call.

### Board
Nadia (CEO), Ravi (CTO), one seat each for Redpoint (Series A) and IVP
(Series B), plus one independent director (Karen Miyoshi, added 2025-Q1).
